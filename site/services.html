Step by Step

The Ornate Experience

1

Free Design Consultation

We visit your property, discuss your vision, and design a custom lighting plan. We look at your rooflines, hedges, trees, walkways, and architectural features to create a display that's uniquely yours. No cookie-cutter templates.

2

Professional Installation

Our licensed team arrives on your scheduled date and installs your display with precision and care. We work efficiently, leave your property clean, and ensure everything is working perfectly before we leave.

3

Season-Long Maintenance

If anything goes out or needs adjustment during the season, we return to fix it — at no additional charge. We own the lights, so their performance is our responsibility. Your display stays perfect all season.

4

Prompt Removal

After the season, we schedule a removal appointment at your convenience. We carefully take down every strand, clip, and fixture — leaving your home exactly as we found it.

5

Safe Storage Until Next Season

Your lights are stored by us in climate-controlled conditions until the following season. No boxes in your basement. No damaged strands. Ready to go every year.
