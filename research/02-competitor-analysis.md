## FIVE PATTERNS ALL TOP SITES SHARE

### Pattern 1: They Sell the Outcome, Not the Service
Top sites: "Come home to a Winter Wonderland." / "Cozy up with cocoa." / "Make your home sparkle."
Average sites: "We install Christmas lights." / "Christmas light installation Fairfield County CT."
**Lesson:** Sell the transformation and feeling, not the labor.

### Pattern 2: Mid-Page Lead Capture
Every top site places a consultation/quote form or CTA within the scrollable homepage body — not hidden in a footer or separate contact page. The CTA comes to the user.
**Lesson:** Don't make customers hunt for how to reach you.

### Pattern 3: A Documented 3–8 Step Process
Every top site shows their process: consultation → design → install → maintenance → removal → storage. This removes anxiety about "strangers on my roof."
**Lesson:** Show how you work, not just what you do.

### Pattern 4: Layered Trust Stacking
Top sites combine: licensed + insured + years in business + warranty + named team + certification/award.
Average sites: "insured" buried in footer.
**Lesson:** Stack trust signals in a dedicated section, not scattered footnotes.

### Pattern 5: Professional Dusk/Evening Photography
Every top site leads with professional blue-hour photography of illuminated homes. Dusk shots are dramatically more emotional and aspirational than daytime utility photos.
**Lesson:** Blue-hour photography is non-negotiable for premium positioning.

## MARKET GAPS (Opportunities for Ornate)

1. **No one owns luxury residential end-to-end locally.** Decorate With Lights is closest but is a NY franchise. A Fairfield County-native premium brand has an open lane.
2. **No competitor positions above mid-market.** Hang My Lights anchors at $499. The $2,000–$8,000+ estate market is completely unaddressed visually.
3. **"Ornate" is the only name that signals craftsmanship.** Every competitor is a landscaping company that does Christmas lighting. Ornate Christmas Lighting is the only brand name built around the service and quality level.
4. **FAQ/objection-handling gap.** Only Summer Rain has a comprehensive FAQ. Most competitors leave common objections unanswered.
5. **No before/after gallery.** Every site shows "after" only. A before/after would be category-defining.
6. **No live Google review widget on homepage.** Nobody prominently shows a star rating count in the hero section.
7. **No video content.** Zero competitors use hero video, drone footage, or time-lapse installation. A 30-second hero video would be completely distinctive.
